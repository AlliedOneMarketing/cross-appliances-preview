What we carry

Everything on our floor is a specific used machine with its own serial number, its own photos and its own condition grade — not a catalog listing. We stock washers, dryers, refrigerators, ranges, dishwashers, freezers and over-the-range microwaves, and what is available changes weekly.

Every unit is graded A, B or C before it goes out, and any dent or scratch is written on the listing. Most carry 90 days on parts and labor.

Delivery to West Fort Worth

Delivery, install and haul-away of your old unit is a $79 flat rate inside Parker County. We run to West Fort Worth most weekdays, and you pick a two-hour arrival window instead of waiting all day. If you would rather collect it yourself, both Weatherford stores load into a truck or trailer at no charge.

Appliance repair in West Fort Worth

We repair what we sell and what we did not. Diagnostic is $89 and is waived if we carry out the repair. Call the repairs line on 817-629-8047 to book a technician, or book online and choose your own window.

Financing

No credit needed. Acima and Snap Finance are both approved in store in about ten minutes — bring a photo ID, a checking account and proof of income. Deposits, delivery fees and service calls can also be paid by card, with no surcharge.

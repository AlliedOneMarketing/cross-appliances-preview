## What actually works

- **Unit-level inventory** — every item is one specific machine with a serial, a condition grade and days-on-floor
- **Staff console** (login required) — 60-second photo intake that publishes straight to the site
- **Pricing engine** — age-based auto-markdown and market-comp, with a hard margin floor
- **Scheduling** — delivery and service booking capped by real truck and tech capacity
- **Payments** — deposits, delivery fees and service calls, with a ledger and financing lead capture

## Important

- Payments are **simulated**. No gateway is connected. Production routes through Accept Blue.
- Inventory, pricing, bookings and transactions are **demonstration data** and reset on page refresh.
- Product images are **rendered stand-ins**, not photographs of real Cross inventory.
- Acima and Snap lockups are **typographic placeholders**, not their trademarked logos.
- Maps are branded locators; directions/call/email links are real.
- The site is `noindex` everywhere and blocked in `robots.txt` — it must never be indexed while it carries Cross Appliances' name, address and phone on a domain that isn't theirs.
